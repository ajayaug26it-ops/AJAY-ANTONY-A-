Plate 02

About

I would rather understand why it runs than copy something that does.

I'm Ajay, a first-year B.Tech student in Information Technology. A few months in, I'm still at the stage where every new idea feels like a door I hadn't noticed before.

I like taking things apart to see how they work. It's slower than following a tutorial word for word, but it means that when something breaks, I'm not completely lost. I'd rather be confused for an hour and understand the answer than be quick and never know why.

Right now I'm building the basics: how programs are structured, how the web fits together, how to ask a good question when I'm stuck. This portfolio is part of that. I made every piece of it by hand, and I wanted it to feel like something a person made, because one did.
